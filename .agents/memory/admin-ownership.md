---
name: Owner-only Admin access
description: Preserve the business-owner-approved simple password flow until the real owner email is ready.
---

Keep Admin access limited to the business owner through the approved server-verified password flow until the owner’s real email identity is ready. Do not broaden access merely because another authentication provider is available.

**Why:** The business owner approved this approach because only they hold the access information and the login experience is deliberately simple.

**How to apply:** Leave the current owner-only flow unchanged. When the real owner email is created, migrate ownership explicitly while preserving exclusive access and the existing simple dashboard experience.
